# Related work

| Source | Connection |
| --- | --- |
| [Bartlett, Rudolph and Spekkens, Rev. Mod. Phys. **79**, 555–609 (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.79.555) | Collective rotation symmetry, twirling, multiplicity spaces and different four-spin singlet pairings underlie the allowed operations. Section III.B uses these pairings as information carriers. Here they are different preparations and readouts in a field-sensing test. |
| [Andersson, Jex and Barnett, J. Phys. A **36**, 2325–2338 (2003)](https://doi.org/10.1088/0305-4470/36/9/310) | A singlet probe can compare two unknown qubit transformations used in parallel. The present triplet of transformations has a reversal-odd, ordered trace component. Two crossed singlet configurations expose that component and permit a quantitative comparison of sheltered spins and sensing windows. |
| [Reascos, Murta, Galvão and Fernández-Rossier, Phys. Rev. Research **5**, 043087 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.5.043087) | The authors give circuits to measure the scalar spin chirality of a supplied state. Here a signed scalar triple product describes unknown local channel generators, and chirality is inferred from changes in the rates of a fixed detector. |
| [Kaubruegger et al., PRX Quantum **4**, 020333 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.4.020333) | Quantum vector-field sensing can estimate field components under a specified apparatus and probe design. The present task restricts preparation and readout to rotation-invariant operations and asks only for handedness. |
| [Collins et al., Phys. Rev. A **72**, 022304 (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.72.022304) | Quantum gloves are rotationally invariant encodings of spatial chirality. Here the sign is attached to an ordered triad of precession vectors and is reversed by inverting all fields. |
| [Qiao et al., Nat. Commun. **11**, 3022 (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-16745-0) and [Wang et al., npj Quantum Inf. **9**, 58 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-023-00727-3) | Four-spin singlet preparation, central exchange and pair readout provide device-level precedents. A field-handedness experiment also requires control of the three field windows, the fourth site's exposure, and biases between the two settings. |
